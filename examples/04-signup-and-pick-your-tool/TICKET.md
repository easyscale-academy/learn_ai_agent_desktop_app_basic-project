# Pick your tool and get your account ready

## 1. Goal

Before you install anything, clear the two things standing in the way: pick which tool you're going to learn, and get its personal account registered for free. Once this is done, all that's left is installing and getting hands-on.

Note that the bar here is set low: the account just needs to log in and send one message, you're not required to add a card or set up a subscription yet.

---

## 2. What to do

1. Read through the README in this directory, especially the section on picking a tool based on your situation, and the three notes about personal accounts and not adding a card yet.
2. Write down which tool you picked, along with a reason grounded in your own situation (where you are, where your career is headed).
3. Confirm you have your own personal email address. Recommendation: whichever tool you pick, get a ChatGPT account set up too, since it's the most broadly useful; if you picked Codex, it logs in with your ChatGPT account anyway.
4. Open the external guide at https://offer-forge-public.easyscalecloud.com/projects/offer_forge_blogs/blogs/3pVkdtyRVJpyHsff/index.html and work through it in order until the account for your chosen tool can log in. You can skip adding a card and setting up a subscription for now.
5. Log in once through the web version and send a message to confirm the account is alive. Running out of free quota after three to five messages is normal.
6. Note down the number of the article you'll read next (Claude Code is 05, Codex is 06, Antigravity is 07), and skip the other two.

**Estimated time:** 10 to 20 minutes to decide; account signup depends on how the external guide goes, usually 1 to 2 hours

---

## 3. Checklist

- [ ] **Decision made**: You've written down which tool you picked, with a reason grounded in where you are and where your career is headed, not a feature comparison.
- [ ] **Understand why signup isn't taught here**: You know that accounts, email, phone numbers, and payment all belong in the external guide, not in this course.
- [ ] **Personal vs. enterprise**: You've confirmed you're following the personal-account path; if your situation calls for an enterprise account instead, you've already told your instructor.
- [ ] **Account is alive**: You've successfully logged in through the web version and sent a message. No card or subscription required.
- [ ] **Habit of naming your tool**: You understand that going forward, you should name both the tool and the form factor you're using when asking questions, not just say you used an Agent.
- [ ] **Path is clear**: You know which article to read next, and that you can skip the other two.
