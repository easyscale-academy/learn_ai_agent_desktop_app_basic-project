# Explain the Relationship Between Foundation Models, ChatBots, and Agents

## 1. Goal

Get these tangled-up terms straight once and for all, and build a skeleton diagram in your head. Once it's in place, every choice you make later in this course — which tool to pick, which subscription to buy, how to install it — will make sense to you, and you won't get dazzled by the next wave of new product names either.

---

## 2. What to Do

1. Read through the README in this directory, paying particular attention to the formula "Agent equals foundation model plus Harness" and the timeline from large model to super app.
2. Find someone who knows nothing about any of this, or talk it out loud to a voice recording. In under three minutes, using plain language the whole time, explain four things: what the brain is, what a ChatBot is, why an Agent beats a ChatBot, and where your money actually goes. Wherever you get stuck, go back and re-read that part.
3. List four or five AI products you've heard of, and for each one answer three questions: is it a ChatBot or an Agent, is the brain behind it its own or rented, and is it CLI or Desktop. If you can't tell, check the product's website.
4. Write one sentence stating where you'd place personal AI operating systems like OpenClaw in this picture, and whether you should bother chasing them right now.

**Estimated time:** 30 to 45 minutes

---

## 3. Checklist

- [ ] **The three-layer relationship**: You can explain what a foundation model, a ChatBot, and an Agent each are, and how they're layered on top of one another.
- [ ] **The core formula**: You can explain "Agent equals foundation model plus Harness," and use it to explain why products without their own model have a thin moat.
- [ ] **The origin story**: You can explain the real reason these Agents all got their start writing code — that programming is the easiest domain to verify right and wrong in on the spot — not that they can only write code.
- [ ] **The two forms**: You can state the difference between CLI and Desktop, and why this course picked Desktop.
- [ ] **What gets billed**: You can explain in one sentence that what gets billed is the brain's compute, and that the software itself is usually free.
- [ ] **Placing products on the map**: You've completed the product-placement exercise, and can use this skeleton to place something new like OpenClaw.
