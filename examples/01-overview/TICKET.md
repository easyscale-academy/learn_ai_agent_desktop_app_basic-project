# Read the Series Index and Get the Map

## 1. Goal

This Task has exactly one job: spend a few minutes going over the map of the whole course, so you know how the eight pieces ahead break down, what each block solves, and what order to walk them in. There's nothing to produce and nothing to run, but it's worth reading properly before you move on, otherwise it's easy to lose your bearings inside the long articles in the middle.
