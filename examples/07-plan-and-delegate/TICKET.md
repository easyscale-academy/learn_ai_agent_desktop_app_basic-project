# Run a Round of AI Work Using the Plan and Parallel Mindset

## 1. Goal

Put the method from Task 07 into practice on real tasks: sort your tasks by type first, write a plan during the exploration stage, then open several sessions at once during the execution stage and review them in rotation. Once you finish this, you will not just know how to use an Agent App, you will know how to use it fast.

---

## 2. What to Do

1. Read through the README in this directory, paying particular attention to the section on classifying tasks and the section on reaching seventy percent maturity before going parallel.
2. Pick two things you genuinely need to get done right now that you have not yet figured out how to approach.
3. Open a separate session for each one. Spend ten or fifteen minutes talking through the goal and steps for each with the AI, stop once you reach seventy percent maturity, and have it write the result into a plan document.
4. Once both have reached seventy percent, open two sessions at the same time and dispatch the first step for each.
5. Switch back and forth reviewing the results from both sessions, advancing each one through at least one full round (dispatch, wait, review, dispatch the next step).
6. Write a one-sentence summary: which part of this was exploration, which part was execution, and how your role changed once you went parallel.

**Estimated time:** 40 to 60 minutes

---

## 3. Checklist

- [ ] **Task classification is clear**: you can state whether each task on your plate is exploratory or execution-and-review, and explain the reasoning behind that call.
- [ ] **Plan document exists for both**: each of the two tasks has its own plan document laying out the goal and steps, and each one was stopped at seventy percent maturity, not pushed further.
- [ ] **Parallel execution actually happened**: you had two sessions open at the same time, each advanced by at least one step, rather than finishing them one after another in sequence.
- [ ] **You can explain the role shift**: you can clearly describe how, during the parallel execution stage, your role shifted from deep thinking to reviewing.
- [ ] **One-sentence summary is written**: you wrote a one-sentence retrospective on how this round split between exploration and execution.
