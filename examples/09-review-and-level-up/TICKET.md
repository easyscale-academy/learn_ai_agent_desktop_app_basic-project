# Run the Shared Traits Checklist Against Your Agent App

## 1. Goal

Verify the eight shared traits and the three level up directions from Task 09's README against the tool you're actually using. Once you've done this, you'll not only be comfortable with one Agent App, you'll know exactly what to look for first the next time you pick up a new one, and where to go on your own once this course stops.

---

## 2. What to Do

1. Read through the README in this directory, focusing on the eight shared traits in sections 2 through 9 and the three level up directions in section 10.
2. Open the tool you got comfortable with in Task 05 or Task 06, and locate each of the eight shared traits in its interface, one by one.
3. Pick one small, real task and walk through the whole flow: bind a folder, confirm the permission mode is on the setting you want, hand off work that reads and writes a file, check context usage partway through, and adjust the model or Effort once.
4. Fork off from the current conversation, try a different approach, and confirm the original conversation is untouched.
5. Pick whichever direction in section 10 appeals to you most, and actually use one of its search terms or its one-line prompt once, to see what turns up.
6. Write one sentence summarizing which of these eight things you hadn't noticed before, and now you have.

**Estimated time:** 30 to 45 minutes

---

## 3. Checklist

- [ ] **All eight shared traits checked off**: In your own tool's interface, you've confirmed, one by one, where and how to use folder binding, the file explorer, reading and writing files and images, permission mode, session and context, model and Effort, Agent Skill, and Fork.
- [ ] **A real task run start to finish**: You used one small, real task to walk the complete flow from binding your territory to getting a result, not just clicking around at a few buttons here and there.
- [ ] **Fork used once**: You actually forked a conversation once, and confirmed the original conversation was unaffected.
- [ ] **Level up directions clearly stated**: You can name the directions available if you want to push further, and you know the most important one is reading the official docs with an AI beside you rather than hunting for more second-hand tutorials.
- [ ] **One door actually opened**: You used at least one of the search terms or one-line prompts from section 10 for real.
- [ ] **One-sentence summary written**: You wrote one sentence recording the most surprising thing you found while working through this checklist.
